Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

About this policy

This policy applies to Oriel, a habit tracker for iPhone made by Zhorzhua Studio

What we collect

Nothing

Oriel has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking

Your habits never reach a server run by Zhorzhua Studio, and we do not sell personal data

Your habits

The habits you add, the days you mark, your finished buildings and your settings are stored on your iPhone

Oriel keeps them in a storage area shared only with its own widgets, so the widgets can show and update your buildings

iCloud

When iCloud is available, Oriel syncs your habits between your devices through your private iCloud database using Apple CloudKit

That database belongs to your Apple Account, and Zhorzhua Studio cannot read it

Apple provides iCloud under its Privacy Policy

Reminders

If you turn on a reminder, iOS schedules it on your device as a local notification

Reminders never pass through a server

Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts

Widgets and Shortcuts actions read and mark your habits on the device

If you speak to Siri, Apple handles the request under its own privacy terms

Oriel Plus

The optional Oriel Plus is bought and managed through the App Store

Apple handles billing under its Privacy Policy, and we never receive your payment details

Oriel checks your access on the device using Apple’s purchase records, with no account or server of ours

Deleting your data

You can archive a habit, or delete it for good from the archive

Deleting the app removes everything Oriel stored on that iPhone

Copies in iCloud stay until you remove them in the iCloud storage settings of your Apple Account

This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives ordinary request information such as your IP address under GitHub’s privacy statement

Changes

If Oriel’s handling of data changes, we will update this page and its date before the change ships
