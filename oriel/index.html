Oriel

Every habit grows a tower

A habit tracker for iPhone that builds a tower for every habit

Each day you keep it lights a window

When a house is finished, the next one goes up on its roof, into the clouds

Get it on the App Store

Free to download, iPhone with iOS 26 or later

What it is

Each day you keep a habit, a window lights up in its house

Every house has its own number of windows, one for each day

When every window has had its day, the house is finished and the next one goes up on its roof

Tap today's window and the light comes on, tap it again to turn it off

A missed day stays a dark window, and the tower keeps going up

Every habit grows its own tower on your street, and no two houses are alike: a bakery, a bookshop, a flower shop, a pharmacy

The sky follows your hour with the real sun and moon, and the seasons pass over the street

Oriel speaks 11 languages, with VoiceOver and large text throughout

Three screens

Free and Plus

Free

  • Three habits, each with its own tower
  • Every widget, on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen
  • iCloud sync between your devices
  • A gentle reminder at the time and on the days you pick
  • Finished houses saved as wallpapers

Oriel Plus

  • A tower for every habit, no limit of three
  • A yearly subscription that starts with a free week
  • Or a single purchase that never renews
  • If Plus ends, every building and window you made stays

One Plus covers up to six people in your Apple Family Sharing group

Questions

Do I need an account?

No sign-up at all

Your towers stay with you and sync through your own iCloud

What if I miss a day?

That window stays dark and the tower keeps going up

Days to come are still a pencil drawing

Can I mark a day without opening the app?

Yes, tap today's window right in a Home Screen widget

The Building widget comes in small, medium and large, and Street shows up to three houses side by side

Lock Screen widgets show your latest days

What does Plus cost?

In the US it is $14.99 a year with a free week first, or $34.99 once for a purchase that never renews

The App Store shows the price in your own currency before you pay

Cancel the subscription any time in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions

What happens if Plus ends?

Every building and window you made stays

Only a new tower past the first three waits until Plus is back

Who can see my habits?

Only you

Your habits never reach a server of ours, and there are no ads and no tracking
