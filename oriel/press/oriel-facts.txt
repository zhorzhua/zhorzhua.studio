Oriel: fact sheet

In short
Oriel is a habit tracker for iPhone where every habit grows a tower
Each day you keep a habit, a window lights up in its house
Every house has its own number of windows, one for each day
When every window has had its day, the house is finished and the next one goes up on its roof
One tap a day: tap today's window and the light comes on
A missed day stays a dark window and the tower keeps going up
Every habit grows its own tower on your street, and no two houses are alike
Widgets light today's window right from the Home Screen, without opening the app
Free: three habits, each with its own tower, every widget and iCloud
Oriel Plus: a tower for every habit, no limit of three

Developer: Zhorzhua Studio, Manorhamilton, Ireland
Released: 7 November 2026
Current version: 1.0
Platform: iPhone, iOS 26 or later
Languages: English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, Ukrainian
Price: free to download, three habits are free
Oriel Plus: a yearly subscription with a free week, or a single purchase that never renews
US prices: $14.99 a year or $34.99 once
If Plus ends, every building and window you made stays
One Plus covers up to six people in an Apple Family Sharing group
Widgets: Building in small, medium and large, Street with up to three houses side by side, Lock Screen widgets with your latest days
Accessibility: VoiceOver and large text throughout
Privacy: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, habits sync through your own iCloud
