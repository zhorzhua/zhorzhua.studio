Until: Countdown Widgets, fact sheet

In short
Until keeps the days you are waiting for in one quiet list and on your Home Screen or Lock Screen
Count down to Christmas, New Year, a spring trip or a baby due date, each on its own quiet line
Each row is typed in a light monospaced font: the name of the day, a row of dots, the number of days left
The nearest day always sits on top, and on the day itself the number becomes today
Turn on every year for birthdays, anniversaries or New Year
Small, medium and large widgets on the Home Screen, plus three Lock Screen styles
At 9:00 on the day itself, a quiet notification brings the name of the day
A clean white page, or pure black in dark mode
