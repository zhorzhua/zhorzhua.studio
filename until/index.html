five screens

what it does

  • One quiet list: the name of the day, a row of dots, the number of days left
  • The nearest day always sits on top, and on the day itself the number becomes today
  • Turn on every year for birthdays, anniversaries or New Year
  • Small, medium and large on the Home Screen, plus three Lock Screen styles
  • At 9:00 on the day itself, a quiet notification brings the name of the day
  • 8 languages, VoiceOver and large text
  • No account, no ads, no tracking: everything stays on your device

price

Until is free for up to three days, with the small widget, the Lock Screen widgets and the morning note

Until Full is one purchase that never renews

It opens any number of days and the medium and large widgets, and Family Sharing is on

The App Store shows the price in your own currency before you pay

help: adding a day

Tap the plus at the bottom, type a name and turn the wheels to the date

Switch on every year for birthdays and anniversaries, the year wheel folds away

A yearly 29 February falls on 28 February in common years

help: changing or deleting

Tap a day in the list to rename it, move the date or delete it

The cross at the top closes without saving

help: widgets

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget and find Until

Left alone, a widget follows the nearest days

To pin particular days, touch and hold the widget, choose Edit Widget and pick them

The small widget and the three Lock Screen styles are free, medium and large come with Until Full

help: the morning note

On the day itself Until sends a quiet note at 9:00 with the name of the day

Until asks about notifications after you save your first day, and you can change it any time in Settings, Notifications, Until

help: until full

It works with Family Sharing

On a new iPhone, open the purchase page and tap restore
