Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

about this policy

This policy applies to Until, a countdown to the days you wait for, made by Zhorzhua Studio for iPhone

what we collect

Nothing

Until has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it does not connect to any server of ours

We do not sell, share or even see personal data

your days

The names and dates you add are stored only on your iPhone

Until keeps them in a storage area shared with its own widgets, so the widgets can show the days on your Home Screen and Lock Screen

Until does not sync through iCloud

the morning note

If you allow notifications, iOS schedules a local notification at 9:00 on the morning of each day

It is created on your iPhone and never passes through a server

until full

The optional one-time purchase Until Full is bought and managed through the App Store

Apple handles billing under its Privacy Policy, and we never receive your payment details

Until checks the purchase on the device using Apple’s purchase records

deleting your data

Tap a day and choose delete to remove it

Deleting the app removes everything Until stored on that iPhone

this website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives ordinary request information such as your IP address under GitHub’s privacy statement

changes

If Until’s handling of data changes, we will update this page and its date before the change ships
