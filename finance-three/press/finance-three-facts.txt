Finance Three: fact sheet

In short
Finance Three is one calm budget for two people or a whole family
Every entry shows up on every phone in the budget
No bank to link and no sign-up: it uses the iCloud account already on each iPhone
The month starts with a plan, and the main screen shows how much you can still spend today
Logging spending is free, forever
Finance Three Plus adds the shared budget, envelopes, the monthly plan and a breakdown by category

Developer: Zhorzhua Studio, Manorhamilton, Ireland

Platform: iPhone, iOS 26 or later
Languages: English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, Ukrainian
