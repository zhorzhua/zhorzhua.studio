Finance Three: fact sheet

In short
Finance Three puts one number on your screen: the cash you can spend today after bills and savings are covered
Tap the Lock Screen widget at the register, add up your receipt right on the keypad, and get on with your day
No bank to link, no account, no ads: your records live on your phone and in iCloud
Logging spending is free, forever
Finance Three Plus adds the monthly plan with the daily number, envelopes, fixed payments with reminders and a breakdown by category
