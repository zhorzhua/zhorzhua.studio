What it is

You and your partner, or the whole family, write down what came in, what went out and what you set aside

Records live on your phone and in iCloud, so there is no account to create and nothing to connect

The month starts with a plan: your income, fixed payments and envelopes

Then the main screen tells you one number: how much you can still spend today

It shows the facts, not a verdict

Finance Three speaks 11 languages and knows 31 currencies

Questions

Do I need to link a bank?

No, you type each entry yourself and it takes a few seconds

Finance Three never asks for bank or card details

Do I need an account?

No sign-up at all

Finance Three uses the iCloud account that is already on your iPhone

How do two of us share one budget?

Create a family budget and invite people through iCloud

Everyone in it sees every entry on their own phone

Each of you still keeps a personal budget, separate and private

What does Plus cost?

In the US it is $3.99 a month or $24.99 a year, and the yearly plan starts with one month free

The App Store shows the price in your own currency before you pay

Cancel any time in Settings › your name › Subscriptions

What happens if Plus ends?

Nothing you created is deleted and it all stays visible

Envelopes and fixed payments pause until Plus is back

Who can see my money?

Only you and the people you invite

We run no server for your budget, and there are no ads and no tracking
