Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

The short version

Quiet collects nothing

What you quit, the day you started and the days you marked stay on your iPhone and never leave it

What stays on your iPhone

Your choice of cigarette or bottle, the date of the last one, the last day you marked, a slip if you had one and whether hard mode is on

This lives in the app's own storage on the device

We have no server and no copy of it

Deleting the app deletes all of it

Notifications

If you allow them, Quiet schedules its reminders on your iPhone

Nothing is sent from us

Purchase

The one time unlock is handled by Apple

We never see your name, email or payment details

Apple tells the app only that the unlock was bought

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no third party code, no account, no tracking across apps or websites

Health

Quiet is not a medical device and its lines are not medical advice

If you drink heavily every day, talk to a doctor before you stop

Children

Quiet is not made for children

Changes

If this ever changes, this page changes first, with a new date at the top
