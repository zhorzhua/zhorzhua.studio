Privacy Policy

Effective 12 September 2026

About this policy

This policy applies to Wallpaper House for iPhone and iPad, developed by Zhorzhua Studio.

Data collection

Wallpaper House does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Basic app state is stored locally on your device. This information is not sent to the developer and is removed when you delete the app.

Wallpaper downloads

Most chapters of wallpapers are delivered on demand through Apple's App Store infrastructure after the app is installed, so that the initial download stays small. These downloads are handled by Apple under its own Privacy Policy. The developer runs no servers and receives no information about what you download or view.

Photos access

When you choose to save a wallpaper, Wallpaper House requests add-only access to Photos and writes the selected image to your photo library. The app does not read or upload your photo library.

Sharing

When you share a wallpaper, the image is passed to the app or service you choose through the standard iOS share sheet. What happens next is governed by that app or service.

Changes

If Wallpaper House's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.
