In short

Wallpaper House is a gallery of iPhone wallpapers arranged as a house of chapters

Each chapter has its own voice, from Scandinavian dioramas and cozy space to brutalist concrete, textures, abstraction and imaginary lands

You spin a drum of chapters, open a collection, look at any wallpaper full screen and save the original to Photos

Browsing is free, and saving comes with House Key

Halloween: the Skeleton Society

For October the house belongs to the Skeleton Society, seven collections of very stylish skeletons

Velvet ghouls with pearls and a poodle, pop art bones, rubber cartoon skeletons with silent film manners, neon toy box skeletons and silver screen skeletons in black and white

Spooky, but dressed for dinner rather than for a horror film
